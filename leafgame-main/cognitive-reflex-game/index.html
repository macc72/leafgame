How to Play

Decide quickly which arrow key (or WASD) to press based on the leaf color:

Orange Leaf

Match movement direction.
Ignore where the tip points.

Green Leaf

Match apex (tip) direction.
Ignore movement direction.

Every 15 correct hits speeds up movement and increases spawn rate.
Incorrect or missed inputs cost 1 life.
